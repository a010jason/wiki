# Organizational Behavior (Colquitt, LePine, Wesson — McGraw-Hill, 2017)

## Bibliographic（推測）

> Colquitt, J. A., LePine, J. A., & Wesson, M. J. (2017). *Organizational Behavior: Improving Performance and Commitment in the Workplace* (5th or 6th ed.). McGraw-Hill Education.

## 教科書的核心案例

Ch1 內案例（Hyundai/Kia 品質逆轉、Nvidia 文化、Wegmans、Google PiLab、Levi's、IKEA、Hilton、Toyota）皆作為「OB practices → firm performance」的證據鏈。^[extracted]

特別 Moses & Jethro 故事（Exodus 18:13-27）：3,500 年前的「組織設計」原型 —— delegate authority、select on character (truthful, not driven by material gain)、daily issues at lowest level、leader focuses on strategy。OB 最原始的智慧。^[inferred]
