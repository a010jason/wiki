# 公民疲乏（OCB 的邊界條件）

## 命名注意

Colquitt 教科書**沒有**使用「citizenship fatigue」這個專有名詞 — 這是學界（Bolino et al. 2015）構念。Colquitt Ch3 最接近的內容是「Loyal Employees vs. Rising Stars」+「skill curve stretching」18-slide 段（slide 1542-1820）。本頁 **Jason 為跨接學界與 Colquitt 課文做的轉譯**。^[inferred]

## Colquitt Ch3 的核心邊界陳述

> "There is no statistical relationship between continuance commitment and citizenship behavior or any aspects of job performance. Therefore, continuance commitment tends to create more of a passive form of loyalty." ^[extracted from Ch3 slide 425-430]

→ **被成本綁住的忠誠不會轉化為 OCB 或績效。**這是 Colquitt 對「無代價彈簧」假說的直接反例。^[inferred]

## Affective Commitment 才驅動 OCB

> "One meta-analysis of 22 studies with more than 6,000 participants revealed a moderately strong correlation between affective commitment and citizenship behavior." ^[extracted from Ch3 slide 314-317]

→ Citizenship behavior 與 **affective commitment**（情感承諾）正相關；與 **continuance commitment**（持續性承諾）零相關。「OCB 反噬」只限於 continuance / behavioral loyalty 形式。^[inferred]

## Loyal Employees Trap（Ch3 slide 1542-1820）

| 類型 | 行為 | 結果 |
|---|---|---|
| **Loyal Employees** | Good performer / 不主動找外部機會 / 把職場當第二個家 | 落入 skill curve stretching |
| **Rising Stars** | 日常工作 + 主動經營職涯成長 / 累積外部 marketability | 跳脫陷阱 |

### 三個 misleading signals（誤以為自己在進步）

> "Career stagnation and loss of marketable skills are very hard to recognize because the stagnant job can keep you very busy making you believe you are making progress, but in reality, you are not." ^[extracted from Ch3 slide 1743-1750]

1. **Excellent performance review with minimal rewards** — 高評價低獎勵 = 可替代性高的信號
2. **成為 group 的 go-to person 卻多年沒學新東西** — 被 pigeonhole
3. **「很忙但無聊」**（busy but bored）— 不再被挑戰

### 殘酷案例

> "It is often the case that loyal employees struggle to get promoted... In financial downturns, companies tend to let go of their loyal employees but try hard to retain their rising stars." ^[extracted from Ch3 slide 1594-1599]

- 公司在裁員時優先放掉 loyal employees、努力留 rising stars
- 新員工拿到比長年忠誠員工高 10-20% 薪水

## 適用邊界

> "Today, blinding loyalty makes employees prone to make one big mistake that has fatal implications on their careers." ^[extracted from Ch3 slide 1614-1617]

- Colquitt 承認**過去**「skills required to do a job effectively remained the same for decades」的時代沒有此 trap
- 此 boundary 僅在「技術 / 工作所需技能 1-2 年迭代一次」的高變動產業最顯著 ^[inferred]
- Skill curve **stretching**（忠誠員工反覆使用舊技能）vs **stacking**（rising stars 持續疊新技能）— Colquitt 用此區分 ^[extracted]

## 對組織端的涵義

- **獎勵 affective commitment 而非 continuance commitment** — 用情感 / 意義驅動忠誠，不用「離職成本」鎖人 ^[inferred]
- 對 loyal employees 提供 **skill stacking 機會**（job rotation / new project / external training）以避免他們落入 trap ^[inferred]
- Voice 過度也是 citizenship fatigue 觸發點：見 [[concepts/EVLN-framework]]
