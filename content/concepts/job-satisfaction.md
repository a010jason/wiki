# Job Satisfaction

**A pleasurable emotional state resulting from the appraisal of one's job or job experiences.**^[extracted] 同時建立在 **cognition**（對工作的計算性判斷）與 **affect**（對工作的情緒反應）兩種基礎上，且 affect 與 cognition 互相 color。

在 OB Integrated Model 中位於 **Individual Mechanisms** 的最頂層（與 stress、motivation、trust/justice/ethics、learning 並列），是預測 [[concepts/job-performance]] 與 [[concepts/organizational-commitment]] 的關鍵中介變項。

## Value-Percept Theory（認知基礎）

`Dissatisfaction = (V_want − V_have) × V_importance`

即使缺口大，若重要性低，dissatisfaction 仍小。^[extracted]

## Five Facets of Job Satisfaction

Overall Job Satisfaction = sum of 5 facet satisfactions（與 Overall 的 correlation 強度，Ironson et al. 1989 + Russell et al. 2004）：^[extracted]

| Facet | r with Overall |
|---|---|
| **Satisfaction with the work itself** | **.70**（最強） |
| Supervision satisfaction | .52 |
| Coworker satisfaction | .51 |
| Promotion satisfaction | .39 |
| Pay satisfaction | .22 |

→ **Pay satisfaction 並非 Overall Satisfaction 的主要驅動子**，反 commonsense。^[extracted]

Promotion satisfaction 分四類：Horizontal / Vertical / **Dry**（責任增但無 benefits 提升，聲譽極差）/ Open vs Closed。

## Job Characteristics Theory — Work Itself 的 mechanism

展開頁：[[concepts/job-characteristics-theory]] — Hackman & Oldham 五核心特質 **VISAF**（Variety / Identity / Significance / Autonomy / Feedback）→ 三 psychological states → Work Itself Satisfaction。受 **Growth Need Strength** moderator 調節。

## Mood and Emotions（affective 基礎）

- **Mood**：mild, extended, undirected（pleasant↔unpleasant × activated↔deactivated 二維 circumplex）
- **Emotion**：intense, brief, directed（6 正向 + 8 負向，Lazarus 1991）
- **Affective Events Theory**：workplace events → affective reactions → 影響 work attitudes + affect-driven behavior

### Emotional Labor

為符合組織 display rules 而 enhance / fake / suppress emotions。三層次：
- **Surface acting** — 裝外顯
- **Deep acting** — 內化感受、empathy
- **Genuine acting** — 情緒本就一致

導致 **cognitive dissonance**（情緒、態度、信念、行為間 mismatch）。緩解需 [[concepts/emotional-intelligence]]。^[extracted]

## 關鍵實證

- **BCG 11,000 workers (2024)**：享受工作者比不享受者 **49% less likely** 想換工作。^[extracted]
- **Hybrid / Remote**：Owl Labs — 88% 認同 flexibility 提升 job satisfaction；75% physical health 改善；75% work-life balance 改善。^[extracted]
- **Prodoscore (30,000 美國用戶, 105M data points)**：pandemic WFH productivity +5%。^[extracted]
- **Coworker support → 健康風險**：研究者原以為長工時、惡老闆傷害健康，結果發現都影響小；**最相關因子是 coworker support** — 同事越 mean，員工死亡風險越高。^[extracted]
- **Job Satisfaction → Job Performance** moderate (~.30)；→ **Organizational Commitment** strong (~.50)，affective + normative 強，continuance 弱。^[extracted]

## 跨文化差異

- Taiwan vs US：台灣人正負情緒體驗都較少且強度低；US 工作者報告更多正情緒、更少負情緒。^[extracted]
- US 推崇 enthusiasm + pride（個人主義）；China / Japan 視 pride 為 undesirable，但視 negative emotions 為 useful and constructive。^[extracted]
- "Service with a smile" 規範各國皆有但執行強度不一（Singapore most reserved on anger to customer；France 較接受；US most likely to display happiness）。^[extracted]

## 案例

- **Activision Blizzard**：員工視「為大眾帶來樂趣」具高 significance；2021/07/28 數百員工 walkout 抗議性騷處理。^[extracted]
- **Twitter (2013) → X (2024)**：CEO 重整 + satisfaction surveys 維持 Glassdoor 高分；2023 Glassdoor 整體下滑至 3.4，CEO 只 15% approval；2024 X work-life 3.1 / culture 3.0。^[extracted]
- **Wal-Mart**：低薪 + 無健保 → 多次組工會嘗試（CA 倉儲），都不成功 — dissatisfaction 引發 union formation。^[extracted]
- **Publix**：above-market starting salaries + promote from within + tuition reimbursement → 全美兩大最佳超市之一。^[extracted]

## Open Questions

- **Anger expression 應壓抑還是釋放？** source 自己做 Point/Counterpoint：壓抑致心理代價 vs BLS 統計 16% fatal workplace injuries 來自 workplace violence。教材未裁定。^[ambiguous]
- 遠距工作數據多來自 vendor（Owl Labs / Prodoscore）— selection bias 風險。^[inferred]
- 跨文化 generalizability 多為 US-sample 推論，validation 有限。
