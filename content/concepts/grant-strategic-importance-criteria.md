# Grant 資源／能力策略重要性 6 維度框架

## 三大 meta-category × 6 維度

> "GRANT: Strategic Importance Criteria — Establishing competitive advantage: Relevance, Scarcity. Sustaining competitive advantage: Durability, Transferability, Replicability. Appropriating competitive advantage: Appropriability" ^[extracted from Ch05 Grant vs Barney slide]

| Meta-category | # | 維度 | 中文 | 對應 VRIO |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Establishing** | 1 | Relevance | 相關性 | Valuable |
| **Establishing** | 2 | Scarcity | 稀有性 | Rare |
| **Sustaining** | 3 | **Durability** | 持久性 | **無對應（Grant 獨有）** |
| **Sustaining** | 4 | Transferability | 移轉性 | Imitable（部分） |
| **Sustaining** | 5 | Replicability | 複製性 | Imitable（部分） |
| **Appropriating** | 6 | Appropriability | 專屬性 | Organization（精神類似） |

> "Durability — No equivalent criterion in VRIO" ^[extracted from Grant Ch05]

→ **Durability 是 Grant 框架的關鍵差別點** — 強調資源／能力**能撐多久**而非僅是「現在多稀有、多難模仿」。^[inferred]

## Appropriability 三大子因素

Grant 把 Appropriability 進一步拆解：^[extracted from Ch05 appraisal diagram]

- **Property rights** — 法律所有權
- **Relative bargaining power** — 相對議價力
- **Embeddedness** — 鑲嵌度

詳見 [[concepts/appropriability]] 完整展開。

## Anchor 案例：Icelandair Group（Grant Ch05）

Grant 用 Icelandair 示範 6 維度評估：^[extracted]

| 分類 | 資源／能力 |
|---|---|
| **Key Strengths** | general management / HR / cabin services / flight operations / marketing / financial resources |
| **Key Weaknesses** | location / route network / landing slots |
| **Superfluous Strength** | maintenance |

→ 策略涵義：用 strengths 抵銷 location / network 劣勢。^[inferred]

## 2×2 框架：Relative Strength × Strategic Importance

Grant Ch05 同章 2×2：^[extracted]

| | 低策略重要性 | 高策略重要性 |
|---|---|---|
| **高相對強度** | Superfluous Strength | **Key Strength** |
| **低相對強度** | Zone of Irrelevance | **Key Weakness** |

→ 兩軸：縱軸是「我們相對競爭對手多強」、橫軸是「這資源對策略多重要」。^[inferred]

## 限制
