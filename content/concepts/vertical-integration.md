# 垂直整合（Vertical Integration）

## Grant 定義（Corporate Scope 維度之一）

> "Business Strategy is concerned with HOW a firm competes within a particular market. Corporate Strategy is concerned with WHERE a firm competes, i.e. the scope of its activities. The dimensions of scope are: vertical scope, geographical scope, product scope" ^[extracted from Grant Ch10]

垂直整合 = 是否把**上下游價值鏈活動**內部執行而非市場契約。Make-vs-buy 為核心問句。

## 方向

| 方向 | 中文 | 例 |
|---|---|---|
| **Backward / Upstream** | 向上整合 | 製造商買原料供應商 |
| **Forward / Downstream** | 向下整合 | 製造商買零售通路 |

Grant 鋼罐（steel cans）價值鏈案例：iron ore → steel → steel strip → can making → canning of food/drink；歷史上 steel-strip 段整合，兩端市場契約。^[extracted]

## 譜系：9 階梯（Formalization × Commitment）

Grant Ch10 把 VI 與 spot transactions 之間視為**連續譜**而非二元：^[extracted]

1. Spot sales/purchases
2. Informal supplier/customer relationships
3. Long-term contracts
4. Agency agreements
5. Franchises
6. Supplier/customer partnerships
7. Joint ventures
8. Build to print
9. **Vertical integration**

中間階段稱 **Quasi-vertical integration** — 結合 market 與 internalization 的優點。^[extracted]

## TCE 整合的時機（Grant Ch10）

> Benefits of VI: "Technical economies from integrating processes…—but doesn't necessarily require common ownership. Avoids transactions costs of market contracts in situations where there are: small numbers of firms, transaction-specific investments, opportunism and strategic misrepresentation, taxes and regulations on market transactions. Superior coordination" ^[extracted]

→ 當這些條件 ✓ 時偏向整合：
- 對手少
- 需要 transaction-specific 投資
- 對手可能機會主義行為
- 市場交易稅／法規重
- 需要更強協調

詳見 [[concepts/transaction-cost-economics]]。

## VI 的成本（Grant Ch10）

> "Differences in optimal scale… Inhibits development of distinctive capabilities. Difficulties of managing strategically different businesses. Incentive problems: lack of 'high-powered' incentives. Limits flexibility… Compounding of risk" ^[extracted]

| # | 成本 | 內涵 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | 不同階段最適規模不同 | 整合難以平衡 |
| 2 | 抑制 distinctive capabilities 發展 | 內部供應 = 無外部競爭壓力 |
| 3 | 策略不同的事業難以管理 | 跨業營運邏輯不同 |
| 4 | 激勵問題 | 內部缺乏 "high-powered" 激勵 |
| 5 | 限制彈性 | 需求 / 技術變動時調整成本高 |
| 6 | 風險加成 | 跨環節風險集中 |

## 案例（Grant Ch10）

| 案例 | 內容 |
|---|---|
| **Ford Focus Electric** | LG（battery）+ Magna（drivetrain）+ Ford（assembly）— specialist 協作 |
| **Tesla** | Battery + drivetrain + assembly **全內部** — 垂直整合 |
| **Sony** | 跨遊戲機 / 消費電子 / 電影（product scope 垂直）|
| **TSMC + SoC** | Foundry 與 chip design 高複雜整合 |
| **Apple / Foxconn** | VI（設計 + 零售） + 市場契約（製造外包）|
| **Samsung Electronics** | Lee/Yun 轉型：用 VI 對抗 Sony「封閉」技術，達成產品廣度 |
| **Ant Financial / Alipay / Yu'e Bao / Zhima Credit** | "用 data 做 VI" — Grant 標註 |

## Chandler 的「Visible Hand」

> Chandler's "visible hand" (administrative coordination by managers) replaces Adam Smith's "invisible hand" of market when administrative coordination is more efficient ^[extracted from Grant Ch10]

→ Chandler 8 命題：解釋為何多單位企業（M-form）取代市場成為現代資本主義主流。^[inferred]

## 時代趨勢：向外、不向內

> "Boundaries between firms and markets are becoming increasingly blurred" ^[extracted from Grant Ch10 closing slide]

近代趨勢**離開 VI** — outsourcing / supplier partnerships / franchising / technology partnerships / inter-firm networks。原因：
- IT 降低協調成本
- 全球供應鏈成熟
- 模組化降低 transaction-specific 投資

→ Apple / Foxconn 模型成為主流。^[inferred]
