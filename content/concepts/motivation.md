# 動機（Motivation）

## Colquitt 定義

> "A set of energetic forces that originates within and outside an employee, initiates work-related effort, and determines its direction, intensity, and persistence." ^[extracted from Ch6 slide 130-132]

> "Motivation is not one thing but rather a set of distinct forces." ^[extracted from Ch6 slide 116-117]

→ Motivation 不是**單一**概念，是**一組獨立的 forces** — 既含內在（sense of purpose / self-confidence）也含外在（goals / incentives）。^[extracted]

## 三大可觀察維度

| 維度 | 中文 | 對應問句 |
|---|---|---|
| **Direction** | 方向 | What do you do? |
| **Intensity** | 強度 | How hard? |
| **Persistence** | 持續性 | How long? |

## Engagement = motivation 的當代同義詞

> "We can think of engagement as a contemporary synonym, more or less, for high levels of intensity and persistence in work effort." ^[extracted from Ch6 slide 143-146]

Engagement = vigor + dedication + absorption。^[extracted]

## Ch6 涵蓋的四大理論

| # | 理論 | 一句話定義 | 獨立 wiki 頁 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Expectancy theory** | Effort → Performance (expectancy) → Outcomes (instrumentality) → Value (valence)；任一為 0 則 motivation = 0 | [[concepts/expectancy-theory]] |
| 2 | **Goal-setting theory** | Difficult + specific goals 最有效 | [[concepts/goal-setting-theory]] |
| 3 | **Equity theory** | Outcomes/inputs ratio 與 comparison other 比對；underreward / overreward 皆驅動「restore equity」行為 | [[concepts/equity-theory]] |
| 4 | **Psychological empowerment** | Intrinsic motivation 來自相信工作對更大目的有貢獻；4 beliefs：meaningfulness / self-determination / competence / impact | （未建頁，可補）|

## Expectancy 三組成（Expectancy / Instrumentality / Valence）

> "Motivation is fostered when the employee believes three things: That effort will result in performance (expectancy); That performance will result in outcomes (instrumentality); That those outcomes will be valuable (valence)." ^[extracted from Ch6 slide 235-240]

> "Motivation is zero if either expectancy, instrumentality, or valence is zero." ^[extracted from Ch6 slide 584]

→ 三組成是**乘法關係**，不是加法 — 任一為零則 motivation 完全為零。^[extracted]

## Self-Efficacy：最強的單一 driver

> "The motivating force with the strongest performance effect is self-efficacy/competence, because people who feel a sense of internal self-confidence tend to outperform those who doubt their capabilities." ^[extracted from Ch6 slide 1254-1257]

Self-efficacy 雖然技術上是 expectancy 的個體前置變項，Colquitt Ch6 把它**獨立講解**並列在動機強度排序第一名。^[extracted]

## Colquitt 的動機強度排序（Ch6 slide 1254-1284）

對任務績效的影響力強到弱：^[extracted]

1. **Self-efficacy / competence**（最強）
2. **Difficult goals**
3. **Expectancy × instrumentality × valence**
4. **Equity perceptions**（最弱）

→ 不是所有動機 force 都等價 — 在實務上 self-efficacy 與 goal difficulty 的 ROI 最高。^[inferred]

## 限制／適用邊界

- Engagement 與 motivation **不能完全劃等號** — Colquitt 把 engagement 定義為 motivation 的高強度高持續性表現，兩者邊界存在但常被混用 ^[extracted from Ch6 slide 142-151]
- Expectancy theory 為「**認知選擇（cognitive choice）**」模型，與行為主義 reinforcement 模型有典範差異；Colquitt 在 Expectancy 段暗示但未深入比較 ^[extracted from Ch6 slide 221-227]
- 動機強度排序是**統計平均**，個體與情境變異大 ^[inferred]
