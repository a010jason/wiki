# 企業市場（B2B）十大特徵

## Business Market 定義

> "Consists of all the organizations that acquire goods and services used in the production of other products or services that are sold, rented, or supplied to others." ^[extracted from Kotler Ch04 p.4]

包含所有為了**生產供銷售、租賃或供應給他人**的產品 / 服務而採購商品與服務的組織。^[extracted]

**Anchor 視覺案例：** Caterpillar (CAT) — Kotler Ch04 p.4「What Is Organizational Buying?」頁的代表。^[extracted]

## 十大特徵

> "Fewer, larger buyers; Close supplier–customer relationships; Professional purchasing; Multiple buying influences; Multiple sales calls; Derived demand; Inelastic demand; Fluctuating demand; Geographically concentrated buyers; Direct purchasing" ^[extracted from Ch04 p.5]

| # | 特徵 | 中文 | 內涵 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Fewer, larger buyers** | 更少但更大的買方 | 少數大客戶佔多數採購量 |
| 2 | **Close supplier–customer relationships** | 緊密的供應商-客戶關係 | 長期合作、深度整合 |
| 3 | **Professional purchasing** | 專業採購 | 採購人員受訓練、依採購政策行事 |
| 4 | **Multiple buying influences** | 多重採購影響者 | 決策涉及多人（→ buying center） |
| 5 | **Multiple sales calls** | 多次銷售拜訪 | 成交週期長，需多次接觸 |
| 6 | **Derived demand** | 衍生需求 | 需求源自消費者市場最終需求；消費市場下行 B2B 隨之變化 |
| 7 | **Inelastic demand** | 無彈性需求 | 總體需求對價格變動反應不大（尤其短期）|
| 8 | **Fluctuating demand** | 波動性需求 | 相較消費市場，工業品需求波動更大（加速器效應）|
| 9 | **Geographically concentrated buyers** | 地理集中性 | B2B 買方常集中於特定地理區域／產業聚落 |
| 10 | **Direct purchasing** | 直接採購 | B2B 買方常跳過中間商、直接向製造商採購 |

## 對行銷的涵義

| 特徵 | B2B 行銷涵義 |
|---|---|
| 1 + 2 | Key Account Management 必要；CRM 投入正比於 LTV |
| 3 | 行銷需提供**理性、技術性、文件化**的素材（不是 emotional appeal）^[inferred] |
| 4 + 5 | 行銷觸及多角色（→ [[concepts/buying-center]]）；漏斗長 |
| 6 + 7 + 8 | B2B forecast 必須先看下游消費市場；不能用 price elasticity 經驗 |
| 9 | 地理 segmentation 比 demographic segmentation 重要 |
| 10 | Channel strategy 短，但需自建 sales force |

^[inferred]

## 限制

- Kotler PPT slide **未討論這 10 項特徵的適用邊界**（數位／SaaS B2B 是否仍適用 #9 地理集中、#10 direct purchasing？）^[extracted as gap]
- 10 項之間的相對重要性 Kotler 未排序 ^[ambiguous]
