# Strategy（策略）

## Definitions of Strategy

四個經典定義（Grant 整理）：^[extracted]

| 來源 | 定義 |
|---|---|
| Wordsmyth Dictionary | a plan, method, or series of actions designed to achieve a specific goal or effect |
| **Alfred Chandler (1962)** | the determination of the long-run **goals** and **objectives** + adoption of **courses of action** + allocation of **resources** necessary for carrying out these goals |
| **Richard Rumelt** *(Good Strategy / Bad Strategy, 2011)* | "a cohesive response to an important challenge" |
| Walt Disney's Peter Pan | "It's a plan of attack" *(illustrative)* |

## Strategy ≠ Tactics

關鍵區分：^[extracted]
- **Strategy** = overall plan for **deploying resources** to establish a favorable position（時間長、調動方向）
- **Tactic** = scheme for a specific **maneuver**（時間短、演習動作）

戰略決策的特徵：
- **Important**
- **Significant resource commitment**
- **Not easily reversible** ← 這條最關鍵

→ 不可逆性是「策略」與「戰術」的真正分水嶺。^[inferred]

## Strategy ≠ Business Plan ≠ Vision

實務常混淆：^[inferred]
- **Vision** = where we want to become（境界）
- **Business plan** = how we'll execute（操作）
- **Strategy** = the **glue** between vision and execution，告訴你**怎麼**從現在到 vision

> Jason 邊註：「Strategy 是『見識謀斷行』」 ^[extracted-from-margin]

## 4 個成功 Strategy 的要素

Grant 用 Queen Elizabeth II vs Lady Gaga 對照案例提煉：^[extracted]

| 元素 | Queen Elizabeth | Lady Gaga |
|---|---|---|
| **Goals**（目標）| 終生獻身君主與國家元首角色 | 透過 Lady Gaga 人格的單一明星追求 |
| **Understanding environment**（理解環境） | 對政治/社會環境警覺、敏感於英國民意 | 新音樂經濟學、社群網路行銷潛力、Gen Y 需求 |
| **Resource appraisal**（資源評估） | 認知缺乏正式權威 → 善用傳統忠誠與非正式影響力 | 利用劇場性、怪誕、設計能力、音樂技巧 |
| **Implementation**（執行） | 堅定承諾、嚴格自律、忠誠團隊 | 持續創造新形象、全心投入角色 |

關鍵公式：^[extracted-from-margin]

> **Do right things + Do things right = Do right things right**

## Why Do Firms Need Strategy?

3 個角色（Grant）：^[extracted]

| 角色 | 效果 |
|---|---|
| **Strategy as Decision Support** | improves quality of decision making |
| **Strategy as Coordination and Communication** | creates consistency and unity |
| **Strategy as Target** | improves performance by setting high aspirations |

→ 因為未來不可知（Jason 邊註），策略幫團隊在不確定性下做一致的決定。^[inferred]

## Strategy 的兩個維度

| Dimension | 問題 | 對應工具 |
|---|---|---|
| **Strategy as Positioning**（present, 競爭現在） | Where are we competing? How are we competing? | Product market scope / Geographical scope / Vertical scope |
| **Strategy as Direction**（future, 準備未來） | What do we want to become / achieve? How to get there? | Vision / Mission / Performance goals / R&D priorities / Growth modes（organic / M&A / alliances）|

→ 沒有 direction 只有 positioning = 「養狗樂園」（Jason 邊註）—— 維持現狀但無未來。^[extracted-from-margin]

→ Position 會變，方向不能變太頻繁。^[inferred]

## Sources of Superior Profitability — 策略的兩層

利潤率 > 資金成本（rate of profit above cost of capital）的兩個來源：^[extracted]

```
Rate of profit above   ──→  Industry Attractiveness   ──→  Corporate Strategy
cost of capital              "Which businesses              "佈局"
                              should we be in?"

                       ──→  Competitive Advantage    ──→  Business Strategy
                              "How should we compete?"      "戰術 / 競爭"
```

詳見 [[concepts/corporate-vs-business-strategy]]。

## Hierarchy（產業層級）

Jason 邊註整理：市場 → 產業 → 產品線 → 產品 ^[extracted-from-margin]

策略要先決定在哪一層運作。

## Evolution of Strategic Management（簡史）

| 年代 | 主流 |
|---|---|
| 1950s | Financial budgeting（operational + DCF capital） |
| 1960s | Corporate planning（中期經濟預測） |
| 1970s | **Emergence of Strategic Management**（industry analysis, competitive positioning） |
| 1980s | **The Quest for Competitive Advantage**（resources & capabilities, shareholder value, refocusing/outsourcing/cost cutting） |
| 1990s | New business models, flexibility, social/env responsibility |
| 2000s+ | **Adapting to Turbulence and Complexity**（VUCA、Red Queen、hyper-competition）|

→ 對應 [[concepts/agile-organization]] 的 paradigm shift。^[inferred]
