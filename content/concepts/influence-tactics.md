# Influence Tactics

Power（[[concepts/power-bases]]）是 capacity；influence tactics 是把 capacity 轉成 behavior 的方式。^[extracted]

Colquitt 整理為 9 tactics × 3 effectiveness levels（依 J.J. Johnson & J.B. Cullen 2002 *Blackwell Handbook of Cross-Cultural Mgmt* 改編）。

## 三層分類

### Most Effective

| Tactic | 內容 |
|---|---|
| **Rational Persuasion** | 用 facts + 邏輯 + 數據 — 唯一在 **upward influence** 一致有效 |
| **Consultation** | 邀對方參與決策 |
| **Inspirational Appeals** | 訴諸對方 values / ideals — 經典如 JFK / Bill Gates vision |
| **Collaboration** | 主動降低 cost of compliance（提供 resources、共同 ownership） |

### Moderately Effective

| Tactic | 內容 |
|---|---|
| **Ingratiation** | "sucking up" — **長期策略才有效**；immediately prior to influence attempt 反成 manipulation |
| **Personal Appeals** | 基於 friendship / loyalty |
| **Exchange** | reward 換 request |
| **Apprising** | 強調 request 對 target **個人**利益（與 rational persuasion 著眼點不同） |

### Least Effective

| Tactic | 內容 |
|---|---|
| **Pressure** | coercive power 透過 threats and demands |
| **Coalitions** | 拉第三方支援 — 通常與其他 tactic 並用 |

## 兩大結論

1. **Tactics 組合使用最有效**
2. **Softer / personal tactics 比 organizational tactics 有效**^[extracted]

## Response to Influence Tactics（三層）

| 反應 | 內容 | 強度 |
|---|---|---|
| **Internalization** | attitude + behavior 都變 | 最強 |
| **Compliance** | 只 behavior 變 + ambivalence | 中 |
| **Resistance** | 拒絕 + 找藉口 + 反向影響 requestor | 失敗（常見於 influencer power 相對低或 request 不合理時） |

## 案例

- **Larry Page / Google engineers**："Larry was fine to be wrong. As long as the data supported the engineers, Larry Page was okay with it" — rational persuasion 強。^[extracted]
- **JFK / FDR / Lincoln / Steve Jobs "Stay Hungry, Stay Foolish"**：inspirational appeals 經典演說。^[extracted]
- **Bill Gates**："computer on every desk and in every home" vision — inspirational。^[extracted]
- **Tony Hsieh / Zappos**：用 inspirational appeal 推 Holacracy。^[extracted]
- **Irene Rosenfeld（前 Mondelez / Kraft CEO）**：softer tactics — 用 facts 反覆說服 + 顯示 support of other people + emotionally and intellectually committed。^[extracted]

## 反例 / 質疑

- **Rational persuasion 失效時機**：當 target 已情感卡死（identity-protective cognition），更多 facts 反讓 target 更頑固。Source 未直接討論。^[inferred]
- **Ingratiation 時機敏感**：長期 OK、即用即丟反效 — 在快節奏遠端工作中拿捏難度增加。^[inferred]
- Hard influence tactics 與 affective commitment **反向作用**：source 自承 organizational forms of power → 降低 affective commitment（反一般直覺）。^[extracted]
