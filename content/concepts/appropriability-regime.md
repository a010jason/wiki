# 專屬性體制（Appropriability Regime）

## Grant 的核心公式

> "Profits from innovation = Value of the innovation to users × Innovator's ability to appropriate the value of the innovation" ^[extracted from Grant Ch09 p.4]

→ 創新即使有 value，**創新者能擷取多少**是另一回事。Appropriability regime 決定後者。

## 四因素（Grant Ch09 p.4）

> The four determinants of appropriability: "Legal protection, Complementary resources, Imitability of the technology, Lead time" ^[extracted]

| # | 因素 | 中文 | 內涵 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Legal protection** | 法律保護 | Patents / copyrights / trademarks / trade secrets + 員工 know-how 限制契約 |
| 2 | **Complementary resources** | 互補資源 | Manufacturing / distribution / finance / service / marketing / 互補技術；議價力取決於互補資源是 generic 還是 specialized |
| 3 | **Imitability of the technology** | 可模仿度 | 競爭者多容易 reverse-engineer |
| 4 | **Lead time** | 領先時間 | First-mover 領先；即使 IP 弱也可能維持優勢 |

## 三條 Pie Chart 對照（Grant Ch09 p.3）

Grant 用三個產業展示 appropriability regime 的差異：^[extracted]

| 產業 | 主要擷取者 | 為何 |
|---|---|---|
| **Pharmaceuticals** | Innovator（製藥廠） | 強 patents、"做不出來" — 高 imitability barriers |
| **Personal Computers** | Customers + complementors | Wintel 標準開放，互補者（Intel / MS）擷取多 |
| **Smartphones** | Followers + complementors | Apple / Samsung（followers）與 app 開發者擷取多，原創者邊緣化 |

## 互補資源的關鍵：Generic vs Specialized

> "Bargaining power of owners of complementary resources depends upon whether complementary resources are generic or specialized" ^[extracted from Ch09 p.5]

- **Generic complementary**（多供應商可提供）→ 創新者議價力強
- **Specialized complementary**（少數玩家壟斷）→ 互補資源持有者搶走利潤

## Lead Time 不是萬靈丹

> "Followers can avoid investing in complementary resources by using better-established industry infrastructure; Firms possessing complementary resources have the luxury of waiting" ^[extracted from Ch09 p.7]

Grant 的 "Timing of Innovation: To Leader or to Follow?" 表顯示：**followers 在許多產品中比 leaders 更常獲勝** — jet airliner / X-ray scanner / office PC / VCR / 微波爐 / web browser / MP3 / e-book reader / social networking。^[extracted]

→ 先進者要保有利潤需要 **IP 保護 + complementary resources + 標準塑造力三者皆強**，否則 fast follower 才是贏家。^[inferred]

## 實證：675 家美國製造商調查（Grant Ch09）

各保護手段的有效性%：^[extracted]

| 手段 | 產品創新保護 | 製程創新保護 |
|---|---:|---:|
| **Secrecy** | 51 | 51 |
| **Lead time** | 53 | 38 |
| Complementary sales / service | 43 | 32 |
| Complementary manufacturing | 38 | 43 |
| Patents | **35** | 23 |
| Trademarks | 27 | n/a |

→ **Secrecy + lead time 普遍勝過 patents**。電子零組件業 patent 只有 18% 效力，secrecy 達 59%（reverse engineering 易）。^[extracted]

## 案例補充（Grant Ch09）

- **ARM** 授權微處理器設計給多家半導體 — 低風險低回報除非 patent 極強 ^[extracted]
- **VHS vs Betamax** — VHS 最大化 market acceptance 贏；Betamax 最大化 value appropriation 輸 ^[extracted]
- **IBM-PC vs Apple Mac** — 開放（高 market acceptance）vs 封閉（高 value appropriation）^[extracted]
- "Why is it so difficult to prevent Chinese firms?" — Grant 標註法律保護在跨國模仿的限制 ^[extracted]
