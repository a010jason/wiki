# Expectancy Theory（Vroom）

[[concepts/motivation]] 的三大主流認知選擇模型之一。Behavior 趨向 pleasure、避開 pain。^[extracted]

## 公式

```
Motivation = (E → P) × Σ[(P → O) × V]
```

- **Expectancy (E→P)**：If I exert effort, will I perform well？
- **Instrumentality (P→O)**：If I perform well, will I receive outcomes？
- **Valence (V)**：anticipated value of outcomes

**乘法效應**：任一為零則整體 motivation 為零。^[extracted]

## Expectancy（E → P）

關鍵決定因素是 **Self-Efficacy**（Bandura 1977, *Psychol Review* 84:191-215）。Self-efficacy 來自 4 source：^[extracted]
1. **Past accomplishments**
2. **Vicarious experience**（看別人做成）
3. **Verbal persuasion**
4. **Emotional cues**（生理喚起的解讀）

→ analysis of task requirements + assessment of personal/situational resources → self-efficacy

**Stajkovic & Luthans (1998)** meta-analysis：self-efficacy 是 motivation 中**最強**的 performance predictor。

## Instrumentality（P → O）

常被四種因素削弱：^[extracted]
- Poor performance measurement
- Inadequate reward budget
- 非績效政策（attendance、seniority）
- Time delays

**現實落差**：10,000+ 員工調查僅 **35%** 認為 performance 驅動 pay — P→O 連結在實務上常斷裂。^[extracted]

## Valence（V）

由 **needs** 驅動。Maslow + Alderfer **ERG** 整合為 5 大 needs：Existence / Relatedness / Control / Esteem / **Meaning**。^[extracted]

| 類型 | 例 |
|---|---|
| **Extrinsic** | Pay / Bonus / Promotion / Status symbols |
| **Intrinsic** | Enjoyment / Knowledge gain / Skill development |

**Meaning of Money** 三象徵（Tang 1992, *JOB* 13:197-202，12-item scale）：Achievement / Respect / Freedom。Men 比 women 更易視 money 為三者；younger employees 比 older 對 money 更負面。^[extracted]

## 案例

- **Netflix**：以 data 識別 salary range 並 **pay at the very top**（whatever combo of salary + stock options 員工偏好）— 強化 P→O。^[extracted]
- **Disney**：員工從 Mickey Mouse 收手寫 appreciation note + cash + stocks + 偶有 car；high performers 可 jump career ladder — 多元 valence + 強 P→O。^[extracted]
- **CEO pay vs shareholder return**：one analysis revealed **near-zero correlation** — 在高階層 instrumentality 設計失效。^[extracted]
- **Kraft Heinz（反例）**：員工抱怨 "corporate leaders... only care about making money off of them" — 低 P→O 認知摧毀 motivation。^[extracted]

## 反例 / 質疑

- **Pay secrecy 矛盾**：36% 公司禁止討論薪資，但 most employees approve pay secrecy — 公平資訊不對稱下 instrumentality 判斷易失真。^[extracted]
- 純認知模型 — 忽略 affective、social context、long-term identity。^[inferred]
- Self-efficacy 自我強化迴圈：成功 → 高 efficacy → 更敢挑戰 → 更多成功；失敗則反向螺旋，可能解釋 stuck performer 難救。^[inferred]
