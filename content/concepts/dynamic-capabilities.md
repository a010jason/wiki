# 動態能力（Dynamic Capabilities）

## Teece 定義（Grant 引述）

> "A firm's ability to integrate, build, and reconfigure internal and external competences to address rapidly changing environments." ^[extracted]
>
> — Teece, quoted in Grant Ch08, p.10

> "Higher order capabilities that orchestrate change among lower-level 'ordinary' or 'operational' capabilities." ^[extracted from Grant Ch08 p.10]

## Teece 三型

> "There are three types of dynamic capability: (1) sensing and shaping of opportunities and threats (2) seizing opportunities (3) maintaining competitiveness through enhancing, combining, protecting, and, when necessary, reconfiguring the enterprise's intangible and tangible assets" ^[extracted from Grant Ch08 p.10]

| # | 類型 | 內涵 |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Sensing & shaping** | 識別並塑造機會與威脅 |
| 2 | **Seizing** | 抓住機會（配置資源、做承諾）|
| 3 | **Maintaining competitiveness** | 強化、組合、保護、必要時 reconfigure 企業的有形 / 無形資產 |

## 為何重要：對付 [[concepts/organizational-inertia]]

Grant Ch08 把 dynamic capabilities 放在「Managing Strategic Change」章節：dynamic capabilities 是**回答 inertia 問題的核心機制**。^[inferred]

→ Ordinary capabilities 解決「日常如何運作」；Dynamic capabilities 解決「能力本身如何更新」。^[inferred]

## 案例（Grant）

| 案例 | 展示內容 | 出處 |
|---|---|---|
| **Honda** | 產品演化 1946–2020：機車 → 汽車 → 船舶引擎 → 燃料電池 → 商用機 | Ch05 |
| **Canon** | 1980–2000：精密機械 + 光學 + 微電子 重組為相機 / 影印機 / 傳真 / 印表機 / 掃描器 | Ch05 |
| **3M** | 1902–2020：黏著劑化學 → 高分子 → 奈米 → 感測器 重組為 Post-it / Scotch tape / 藥物傳遞 / LED 照明 | Ch05 |
| **Hyundai Motor** | 1968–1995 product sequencing：SKD/CKD → Pony → Excel → Alpha engine → Accent/Sonata，逐步加 casting / forging / FWD engineering / hydrodynamics | Ch08 |

## 限制／適用邊界

- Grant 把 dynamic capabilities 放在 Strategic Change 章節 → 隱含**前提：環境變動足夠劇烈**才需要 ^[inferred]
- 穩定產業中 ordinary capabilities 已足 ^[inferred from Grant positioning]
- Grant：「capacity of the existing leadership to initiate change is limited by investment in the status quo and lack of cognitive flexibility」→ 可能需要 **new leadership**；Grant 未明說 dynamic capabilities 能否在不換領導層的情況下完全克服 inertia ^[extracted as ambiguity]
