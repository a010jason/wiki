# Team Interdependence

「Team 之所以是 team」的核心區分軸。包含 task、goal、outcome 三類互賴度，每類獨立可衡量。^[extracted]

OB Integrated Model 中屬 **Group Mechanisms** 層；team interdependence 決定 [[concepts/team-processes|team processes]] 的協調負荷。

## 1. Task Interdependence（4 levels，coordination 需求由低到高）

| Level | 結構 | 範例 |
|---|---|---|
| **Pooled** | 成員各自輸出彙總 | pole-and-line tuna fishing |
| **Sequential** | A → B → C → ... assembly line | car manufacturing |
| **Reciprocal** | 雙向反覆 A ↔ B | customized design project |
| **Comprehensive** | 全員雙向互動 | IDEO 設計團隊 |

## 2. Goal Interdependence

成員共享 team goal 的 shared vision。Driver = **formalized mission statement**（MacMillan 2001 *The Performance Factor*, pp.51-53）8 步驟 + 5 criteria：**Clarity / Relevance / Significance / Believability / Urgency**。^[extracted]

範例 mission statements：^[extracted]
- Coke "refresh the world in body, mind and spirit"
- JetBlue "To inspire humanity — both in the air and on the ground"
- Patagonia "Build the best product, cause no unnecessary harm, use business to inspire and implement solutions to the environmental crisis"

## 3. Outcome Interdependence

成員共享 rewards（pay / bonus / recognition / extra time off / team survival）。^[extracted]

**Mechanism**：提高 outcome interdependence → 增加成員資訊分享 → promotes learning → 提升 team performance。

## 量表

Campion, Papper & Medsker (1996) *Personnel Psychology* 49:429-52 — 9 題（task / goal / outcome interdependence 各 3 題），7 分 totally disagree → totally agree。^[extracted]

## 關鍵實證

- **Campion / Medsker / Higgs (1993)** *Personnel Psychology* 46:823-49 + **Stewart (2006)** *Journal of Management* 32:29-54 meta-analysis：Task interdependence → Team Performance **中度正相關 ~.30**（複雜知識工作更高）；Task interdependence → Team Commitment 弱相關 ~.10。^[extracted]
- High interdependence 增加溝通成本 → productivity 下降；但增強 adaptability 與 novel problem-solving。Trade-off 由 task complexity 決定。^[extracted]

## 案例

- **Mayo Clinic**：每位新病人來時組成新的專家團隊，成員依案例特性動態組合 — comprehensive interdependence。^[extracted]
- **Ford Motor mass production**：sequential interdependence 的歷史 prototype。^[extracted]
- **SEAL Team Six**（擊殺賓拉登 60 Minutes 引用）：Obama 引用 "voiced doubts about this approach was invaluable" — 高互賴 + 高 voice。^[extracted]
- **Logitech**："follow the sun" 全球虛擬 team — 把 time-zone 變優勢。^[extracted]
- **U.S. Navy SEALs**：months of rigorous training 學如何讓個人技能服務 team effort — 同時 submerge own identity 與達成個人 + group goals。^[extracted]

## 反例 / 質疑

- **Sports team 不等於 work team**（Colquitt Point/Counterpoint）：
  - 2010 U.S. Winter Olympic Team — hockey / figure skating / curling 之間幾乎無互動；Shani Davis 不與隊伍同住同練 — 拒絕承認運動「團隊」是真 team。^[extracted]
  - **Swimming（除接力外）不是 team**：總成績只是個人成績加總 — pooled 偽 team。^[extracted]
  - Soccer 才是真 team（reciprocal + comprehensive）。
- **三 test 才該用 team**：(1) 工作能否多人做得更好（要 complex + 需多元觀點）(2) 工作是否創造超出個人加總的 common purpose (3) members 是否真的 interdependent。否則 team 反成本。^[extracted]
