# Transformational Leadership

Leader inspires followers to commit to a **shared vision**、做 role model、help reach potential、view problems from new perspectives；把短期 self-interest 轉成 collective good，使 perform beyond expectations。^[extracted]

在 OB Integrated Model 中位於 **Group Mechanisms** 層；Bass & Riggio 2006《Transformational Leadership》2nd ed. 為理論主源。

## 4 I 維度

| I | 名稱 | 內容 | 經典演說 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **I1** | **Idealized Influence** | charisma — "divinely inspired gift" | Lincoln Gettysburg "Fourscore and seven years ago..." |
| **I2** | **Inspirational Motivation** | "meaning making" — 凸顯 status quo 負面、未來正面 | FDR "the only thing we have to fear is fear itself" |
| **I3** | **Intellectual Stimulation** | challenge assumptions, reframe old situations | JFK "ask not what your country can do for you" |
| **I4** | **Individualized Consideration** | coaching、mentoring、個別化對待 | — |

## Full Range Leadership Spectrum（passive → active × ineffective → effective）

```
Laissez-Faire < Passive MBE < Active MBE < Contingent Reward < Transformational
```

- **Laissez-faire** = absence of action，最 ineffective
- **Transactional**（contingent reward + MBE active/passive）= carrot-and-stick
- **Transformational** 是最有效但**並非取代** transactional — 通常疊加運作

## 關鍵實證

- **Transformational → Job Performance** ~.30（中度正向）：Task Performance + Citizenship Behavior 提升；對 [[concepts/counterproductive-workplace-behavior|CWB]] 效果不明（source 明寫）。^[extracted]
- **Transformational → Organizational Commitment** ~.50（**強**正向）：Affective + Normative Commitment 提升；對 **Continuance Commitment 無效**。^[extracted]
- Source: Judge & Piccolo 2004, *JAP* 89:755-68；Meyer et al. 2002, *JVB* 61:20-52；Podsakoff et al. 2000, *J Mgmt* 26:513-63。
- **Charisma genetic**：**60% of variation** in charismatic behavior 可由 genes 解釋（中介路徑為 extraversion + openness + agreeableness 三 trait 的遺傳成分）。^[extracted]
- **Crisis 強化 transformational**：times of crisis / stress / turbulence → people long for charismatic leaders；idealistic vision 在此時 resonance 更深。^[extracted]
- **Contingent reward** meta-analysis (87 studies)：與 follower motivation + 評估 leader effectiveness 強相關。^[extracted]

## 案例

- **Elon Musk（Tesla / SpaceX）**：transformational + autocratic + transactional 混合 —
  - Transformational：火星可重用火箭願景；"People work better when they know what the goal is and why"
  - Autocratic / transactional：2018 Wired 前主管 "Everyone in Tesla is in an abusive relationship with Elon"；自稱 "nano-manager"
  - 但**非純 dictator**：90% 的時候是對的；員工「came out of it 10 times smarter」
  - 結論："he demands excellence because it is what drives transformation"
- **Steve Jobs**："My job is not to be easy on people. My job is to take these great people we have and to push them and make them even better." — idealized influence + individualized consideration。^[extracted]
- **Winston Churchill（*Darkest Hour*）**：politics 為 transformational leadership 經典場域，4 I 全展示。^[extracted]
- **Hamdi Ulukaya（Chobani CEO）**：high-structure + high-consideration；「I'm a shepherd and I'm a warrior — I come and go between those two.」^[extracted]

## 對比 Authentic / Servant Leadership

- **Authentic Leadership（Neider & Schriesheim 2011, 4D）**：Self-awareness / Relational transparency / Balanced processing / Internalized moral perspective → 核心產出 **trust**
- **Servant Leadership**：超越 self-interest，focus on opportunities to help followers grow；listening、empathizing、persuading、stewardship、developing followers' potential
- 跨文化線索：East Asian leader prototype（被畫在 group 後方收集意見）更接近 **servant** style，這型在 collectivistic 文化更 effective。^[extracted]

## 反例 / 質疑

- **Transformational 對 Continuance Commitment 無效**：transformational 不能讓員工因「leaving cost 高」而留下 — 只能透過 affective / normative 路徑。^[extracted]
- **Transformational 對 CWB 證據薄弱**：source 明寫「Less is known about the effects of transformational leadership on Counterproductive Behavior」。^[extracted]
- **Charisma 的遺傳論限制**：60% genetic 暗示 transformational leadership 的**可訓練性有上限**（trait-bound）。^[extracted]
- **Crisis 才 conducive**：transformational leadership emergence 需要 crisis / stress / turbulence — 平時 emergence 機率較低，是 boundary condition。^[extracted]
- **Musk-style toxic 邊緣**：source 自承「awe-inspiring but can also verge on being borderline toxic」；transformational + autocratic + transactional 混合可能造成 massive organizational problems "when left unchecked"。^[extracted]
