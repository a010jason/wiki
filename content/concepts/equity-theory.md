# Equity Theory（J.S. Adams 1965）

Motivation 不只看自己的 input/outcome，**也看 comparison other**。員工建立 mental ledger 比較自己與他人的「投入 / 產出比」。^[extracted]

Source: Adams, J.S. (1965). "Inequity in Social Exchange," *Advances in Experimental Social Psychology* Vol. 2, pp.267-99.

## Mental Ledger

| Outcomes | Inputs |
|---|---|
| Pay / Bonuses | Effort |
| Seniority benefits | Performance |
| Fringe benefits | Skills & abilities |
| Status symbols | Education |
| Satisfying supervision | Experience |
| Workplace perks | Training |
| Intrinsic rewards | Seniority |

## 三狀態與三反應

| 狀態 | 公式 | 情緒 | 行為 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Equity** | Yours = Other's | 中性 | 無動作 |
| **Underreward** | Yours < Other's | Anger | grow own outcomes（談判 / 偷竊）或 shrink own inputs（降 effort） |
| **Overreward** | Yours > Other's | Guilt | 罕見 shrink outcomes；多透過 cognitive distortion 增 input 合理化 |

**第三條路**：change comparison other — 5 種比較類型：Job / Company / Occupational / Educational / Age Equity。^[extracted]

## 關鍵實證 / 數據

- **1,500 員工調查**：65% 計劃 3 個月內換工作，57% 因覺得 underpaid。但 **Salary.com 估計實際 underpaid 僅 19%** — equity 判斷的客觀性差距明顯。^[extracted]
- **CEO pay ratio**：1980 median CEO comp = 33× average worker → 三十年後 > **100×**；one analysis revealed **near-zero correlation** between CEO pay and shareholder returns。^[extracted]
- **Pay secrecy**：36% 公司明確 discourage 員工討論薪資；多數員工 approve pay secrecy。^[extracted]
- **Meta-analyses**：equity 對 [[concepts/organizational-citizenship-behavior]] 中度正向、對 [[concepts/counterproductive-workplace-behavior]] 中度負向；對 affective + normative commitment 中度，對 continuance 弱。^[extracted]

## 案例

- **BBC 2018 Carrie Gracie**：辭職抗議「secretive and illegal pay culture」「crisis of trust」— 男性國際 editor 比女性 counterpart 高 ≥50%。^[extracted]
- **Iceland 2018 法律**：24 人以上公司須政府認證同工同酬，否則社群曝光 + 罰款 — 把 distributive equity 法制化。^[extracted]
- **Equal Pay Pledge**（Accenture / Airbnb / Care.com）：把 equity 列入 hiring → milestones → equal pay 流程設計。^[extracted]

## 反例 / 質疑

- **Cognitive distortion**：Overreward 狀態下 source 自嘲「Shrink your outcomes (yeah, right!...)」— 員工不會真降 outcomes，多以扭曲認知合理化。質疑 equity 是否真的「驅動行為」還是只是「事後合理化」。^[extracted]
- **Equity perception ≠ 客觀現實**：57% 自認 underpaid vs 19% 實際 underpaid。同一事實兩種解讀。
- **Pay secrecy 矛盾**：員工同時抱怨缺資訊但又 approve secrecy — 信任成本高過 transparency 收益。^[extracted]
- 跨文化：individualism vs collectivism + merit vs equality reward 規範差異大。^[extracted]

## 與 Organizational Justice 的關係

Equity Theory（1965）= **Distributive Justice**（結果公平）的祖先。後續延伸到流程、人際、資訊三維 → 見 [[concepts/organizational-justice]]。^[inferred]
