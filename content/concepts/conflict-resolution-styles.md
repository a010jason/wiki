# Conflict Resolution Styles（Thomas-Kilmann）

雙軸：**Assertiveness**（concern for own outcomes）× **Cooperativeness**（concern for other's outcomes）→ 5 styles。^[extracted]

## 五種 style

| Style | Assert | Coop | 結果 | 適用 |
|---|---|---|---|---|
| **Competing** | High | Low | win-lose | emergencies、不受歡迎決策、確信自己對時 |
| **Avoiding** | Low | Low | lose-lose | 議題瑣碎、需冷靜、潛在 disruption 大於收益 |
| **Accommodating** | Low | High | lose-win | issue 對對方更重要、自己 power 較低 |
| **Collaboration** | High | High | win-win | 最難達成 — 需 full information sharing + equal power + 時間投入 |
| **Compromise** | Mid | Mid | give-and-take | **女性比男性更傾向用** |

## 沒有 universal best style

source 自承：「It is a mistake to think that one specific style is superior to another — research has shown that whether a certain style is effective is dependent on lots of situational issues」。^[extracted]

Trust 在 cooperative forms（accommodating / collaboration）中特別重要。

## Negotiation 的延伸

Negotiation = 「two or more interdependent individuals discuss and attempt to come to an agreement about their different preferences」。^[extracted]

| 策略 | 條件 |
|---|---|
| **Distributive bargaining** | zero-sum / fixed-pie（如 union-management labor negotiations） |
| **Integrative bargaining** | win-win — 需 multiple outcomes possible + trust + flexibility |

**4 Stages**：Preparation（含 **BATNA** = Best Alternative To a Negotiated Agreement）→ Exchanging information → Bargaining → Closing and commitment。^[extracted]

## Negotiation 兩大 biases

1. **Perceived power relationship**：自覺較有 power → 要更多、讓更少、更 aggressive → distributive approach^[extracted]
2. **Negotiator emotions**：positive → integrative 但 **overconfident 太快決定**；negative → distributive + **lower judgment accuracy**^[extracted]

## 案例

- **Ken Lay / Enron**：Competing style（high assert / low coop）= win-lose 範例。^[extracted]
- **Bridge of Spies / Donovan（Tom Hanks）**：與東德 hardball 談判 — 對美國價值高 + 對方會利用 weakness。^[extracted]

## 反例 / 質疑

- **Gender difference 推論性**：「Women are more likely to use compromise... men are more likely to use competing」— source 未討論該差異是個人特質還是社會結構造成。^[extracted]
- **Collaboration 條件高**：需 equal power + trust + time — 多數職場無法達成，因此 best style 在 paper 但 rare in practice。^[inferred]
- Avoiding 並非永遠負面 — 短期 cool-down 有戰略價值；source 自承「議題瑣碎、disruption > 收益時」適用。^[extracted]
