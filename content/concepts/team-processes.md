# 團隊歷程（Team Processes）

## 定義

> "Team processes refer to the different types of activities and interactions that occur within teams and contribute to their ultimate end goals. Team processes include interactions among members that occur behaviorally, as well as the hard-to-see feelings and thoughts that coalesce as a consequence of member interactions." ^[extracted from Ch12 slide 54-63]

> "Team processes, in turn, have a strong impact on team effectiveness." ^[extracted from Ch12 slide 74-75]

## Process Gain vs Process Loss

> "Process gain is getting more from the team than you would expect according to the capabilities of its individual members... synonymous with 'synergy.'" ^[extracted from Ch12 slide 94-117]

| Process Loss 來源 | 機制 |
|---|---|
| **Coordination loss** | 含 production blocking — 成員互相打斷 |
| **Motivational loss** | 含 social loafing — 搭便車 |

Steiner (1972) 的 "process loss" 經典引用，特別針對 coordination breakdown。^[extracted from Ch12 slide 711-713]

## 兩大支線

| 支線 | 中文 | 內容 |
|---|---|---|
| **Taskwork processes** | 任務歷程 | 直接執行任務的活動：creative behavior / decision making / boundary spanning |
| **Teamwork processes** | 團隊合作歷程 | 不直接執行任務、但塑造任務情境的人際活動 |

> "Teamwork processes refer to the interpersonal activities that facilitate the accomplishment of the team's work, but do not directly involve task accomplishment itself." ^[extracted from Ch12 slide 529-531]

## Teamwork 三類（Marks, Mathieu, & Zaccaro 2001 Taxonomy）

### 1. Transition Processes（任務週期切換時）

> "Transition processes are teamwork activities that focus on preparation for future work." ^[extracted from Ch12 slide 550-551]

- **Mission analysis** — 任務分析
- **Goal specification** — 目標設定
- **Strategy formulation and planning** — 策略制定 + 應變計畫

例：鏟雪團隊 goal specification「在當日結束前剷除 100% 高速公路 + 50% 鄰里街道」^[extracted from Ch12 slide 590-592]

### 2. Action Processes（任務執行中）

> "Whereas transition processes are important before and between periods of taskwork, action processes are important as the taskwork is being accomplished." ^[extracted from Ch12 slide 628-630]

- **Monitoring progress toward goals** — 進度監控
- **Systems monitoring** — 內部 + 外部資源 / 環境監控
- **Helping behavior / team monitoring and backup responses** — 互助 + 補位
- **Coordination** — 同步化團隊成員活動

> "Coordination refers to synchronizing team members' activities in a way that makes them mesh effectively and seamlessly. The more interdependent the tasks, the more teams rely on coordination as a central process for effective functioning (Tesluk, Mathieu, Zaccaro, & Marks, 1997)." ^[extracted from Ch12 slide 694-699]

例：飛行機艙 helping behavior — 副機師必須持續掌握機長動作以補位。^[extracted from Ch12 slide 683-687]

### 3. Interpersonal Processes（任務前中後皆會發生）

> "Interpersonal processes are important before, during, or between periods of taskwork, and each relates to the manner in which team members manage their relationships." ^[extracted from Ch12 slide 721-723]

- **Conflict management** — 區分 relationship conflict 與 task conflict
- **Motivating and confidence building**
- **Affect management** — 規範成員情緒（開玩笑、放鬆、抱怨）

## Task Conflict 的三條件

Colquitt：**task conflict 只在三條件下有益**：^[extracted from Ch12 slide 770-779]

1. 成員 trust 彼此並能安全表達意見
2. 團隊有有效的 conflict management 實務
3. 成員情緒穩定或對新經驗開放

## Cohesion 不是越高越好

> Cohesion peaked at a certain point, and went [反轉] ^[extracted from Ch12 slide 1067-1072]

「The Dangers of Too Much Workplace Cohesion」— Jeff Bezos "to social cohesion: drop dead" 案例為證（slide 1017+）。^[extracted]

Brainstorming 也是反例：^[extracted from Ch12 slide 256-258]

> "It is surprising that brainstorming rarely works. Team members would be better off coming up with ideas on their own, as individuals, before pooling those ideas"

→ Action process 中的「共同產出」會吃掉個體創造力。^[inferred]

## 限制／適用邊界

- 多國團隊有額外 process problems（語言、溝通、決策流程），需明確 process intervention ^[extracted from Ch12 slide 459-467]
- Cohesion 非 monotonic — 超過某個 sweet spot 反而傷害決策品質 ^[extracted]
- Task conflict 三條件未滿足時，task conflict 退化為 relationship conflict ^[inferred]
