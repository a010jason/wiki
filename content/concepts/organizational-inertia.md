# 組織慣性／組織惰性（Organizational Inertia）

## 五大來源（Grant Ch08 p.6）

> "Organizational Adaptation and Change: The Sources of Inertia"

| # | 來源 | 中文 | Grant 原文 |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **Organizational Routines** | 既有 routines | "Existing patterns of coordinated activity make it difficult to develop new capabilities" |
| 2 | **Social & political structures** | 社會與政治結構 | "Change threatens existing social relationships and power structures" |
| 3 | **Conformity** | 從眾 / 模仿同形 | "Imitation locks firms into common structures and strategies ('institutional isomorphism')" |
| 4 | **Limited Search** | 有限搜尋 | "'Bounded rationality' encourages local search; this is reinforced by managers' contentment with satisfactory rather than optimal solutions" |
| 5 | **Complementarities** | 互補性鎖死 | "Firms create unique configurations of close-fitting organizational features — localized changes tend to be dysfunctional, while systematic change difficult" |

^[extracted from Grant Ch08 p.6]

## 為何重要

> "Capacity of the existing leadership to initiate change is limited by investment in the status quo and lack of cognitive flexibility — hence, the need for new leadership" ^[extracted from Grant Ch08]

→ Grant 把 inertia 不只看作結構問題，**還是領導心理問題** — 既有領導層被 status quo 鎖住，常需 new leadership 才能破局。^[inferred]

## 實證：100 年大公司翻轉

World's Biggest Companies by Market Cap 1912 vs 2018 — **只有 US Steel → Exxon** 在頂部存活，其餘全部翻牌。^[extracted]

US Auto Industry 1895–1960 (Klepper data)：高峰 ~250 家 (1915) → 1955 剩個位數 — shakeout 部分由 incumbent inertia 驅動。^[extracted]

## 技術變革類型決定 inertia 殺傷力

Grant Ch08 區分（不同類型觸發不同 inertia）：^[extracted]

| 二分軸 | 殺傷力較低 | 殺傷力較高 |
|---|---|---|
| Competence-Enhancing vs **Destroying** | Enhancing | **Destroying** |
| Component vs **Architectural** | Component | **Architectural** |
| Sustaining vs **Disruptive** | Sustaining | **Disruptive** |

→ Established firms 在 **Destroying / Architectural / Disruptive** 變革面前 inertia 最致命。

## 限制／適用邊界

- inertia 不是絕對：Grant Hyundai product-sequencing 案例證明公司**能**逐步建新能力 — inertia 是 path-dependent 而非 terminal ^[extracted]
- Grant 把 inertia 定位在「Industry Evolution and Strategic Change」章節 → 暗示 inertia 在**穩定產業**較不致命；在快速變動產業才暴露問題 ^[inferred]
