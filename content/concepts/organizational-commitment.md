# Organizational Commitment

Colquitt-LePine-Wesson 定義：員工對組織的 **(1) desire to remain** + **(2) willingness to exert high effort** + **(3) belief in and acceptance of organizational values**。^[extracted]

在 OB Integrated Model 中與 [[concepts/job-performance]] 並列為 **Individual Outcomes** 兩項產出。Commitment 低 → withdrawal behavior，最終 → quitting。^[extracted]

## 三類承諾（Allen & Meyer 1990）

| 類型 | 動機 | 來源 | 與 OCB / Performance 關聯 |
|---|---|---|---|
| **Affective**（情感型） | "I **want** to" | emotional attachment、同事連結、文化 | 中強正相關 |
| **Continuance**（持續型） | "I **need** to" | sunk cost + 缺 alternatives | **無統計關係**（passive loyalty） |
| **Normative**（規範型） | "I **ought** to" | moral duty + indebted obligation | 與文化背景相關（集體主義國家更強） |

延伸概念 — **embeddedness**（Mitchell et al. 2001, *AMJ* 44:1102-21）：把 continuance commitment 操作化為 Links/Fit/Sacrifice × Organization/Community 六格矩陣，把員工同時用工作與非工作生活鎖在當地。^[extracted]

## Withdrawal behavior（commitment 低的行為產出）

兩大類，moderately to strongly correlated，呈現 **progression**：lateness → absenteeism → quitting。^[extracted]

- **Psychological withdrawal**（= Neglect）：Daydreaming / Socializing / Looking Busy / Cyberloafing / Moonlighting。一份研究指 49% 工作時間花在這些。^[extracted]
- **Physical withdrawal**（= Exit）：Tardiness / Long Breaks / Missing Meetings / Absenteeism / Quitting。

對應 commitment outcome 的反應模型見 [[concepts/EVLN-framework]]。

## Stars / Citizens / Lone Wolves / Apathetics（Griffeth-Gaertner-Sager 1999）

Organizational Commitment × Task Performance 兩軸四象限。預測 EVLN 反應：

| | High Performance | Low Performance |
|---|---|---|
| **High Commitment** | Stars → Voice | Citizens → Loyalty |
| **Low Commitment** | Lone Wolves → Exit | Apathetics → Neglect |

## 關鍵實證

- **US 全產業 turnover ~16%**；單次離職成本 **90%-200% of annual salary**（3% pre-departure / 11% selection / 14% orientation / 20% recruiting / 52% productivity loss）。^[extracted]
- **2024 engagement stats**：85% 員工 not engaged at work；fully engaged 員工離職機率低 87%；高 engagement 企業 profitability +23%（Gallup）。^[extracted]
- **SAS Institute** turnover 4-5% vs 業界 12-20%；長期 high commitment 標竿（pool / child care / 35h workweek）。^[extracted]
- **Cultural moderator**：normative commitment 在 collectivistic cultures（Brazil、China）顯著強於 individualistic cultures（US、Germany）。^[extracted]

## Perceived Organizational Support (POS)

員工感知到組織重視其貢獻、關心其 well-being。組織透過 adequate rewards / job security / minimizing politics / career paths / training opportunities 提升 POS。^[extracted]

## 趨勢與 Psychological Contract

員工-雇主關係從 **relational contracts**（員工欠 loyalty、組織欠 job security/development）轉向 **transactional contracts**（純金錢義務交換）。downsizing 趨勢加速此轉變。^[extracted]

## 反例 / Open Questions

- **Eastman Kodak**：median tenure 20 年（Fortune 500 最高）但只 45% high job satisfaction → tenure 長 ≠ commitment 高，可能只是 continuance（沒地方去）。^[extracted]
- **Japanese corporate samurai 反例**：normative + affective commitment 過頭 → 員工掩蓋 wrongdoing、安全違規、coverup → 日本立法保護 whistleblowers from being fired。^[extracted]
- **章節結尾自我反駁（"One Mistake Loyal Employees Make That Ultimately End Their Careers"）**：從**員工視角**質疑高 commitment：loyal employees vs rising stars 對公司貢獻相同但 career trajectory 顯著不同；技術變遷加速時 "smartest person in the room 太久 = wrong room"。^[extracted]
- 高 affective + 高 normative 可能 trap 員工於停滯職涯。Open question：commitment 的「健康上限」在哪？
